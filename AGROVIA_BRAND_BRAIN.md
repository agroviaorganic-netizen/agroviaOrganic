## 📦 PART 2: THE 5 CORE PRODUCT MONOGRAPHS & SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. Sundarbans Wild Raw Mangrove Honey
* **Origin:** UNESCO World Heritage Sundarbans Mangrove Forest (Khulna/Satkhira).
* **Botanical Flora:** Khalshi, Goran, Bain, Gewa, Keora nectar.
* **Export Lab Standards:** Moisture <= 18.5%, HMF < 15 mg/kg, C4 Sugar Adulteration: 0.000% (IRMS method), Chloramphenicol/Nitrofurans: Not Detected (ND).
* **Packaging:** 200L HDPE food-grade drums (bulk) & 250g/500g/1kg premium amber glass jars with tamper-evident seal and batch-specific QR codes.

### 2. Aromatic & Specialty Rice (Chinigura & GI Tulshimala)
* **Varieties:** Premium Chinigura (Dinajpur) & GI-Certified Tulshimala (Sherpur).
* **Export Standards:** Broken Grains < 3%, Moisture Content < 12.5%, Foreign Matter < 0.1%, 100% Fumigated (Phostoxin gas treatment).
* **Packaging:** 1kg, 2kg, 5kg multi-layer vacuum sealed metallic barrier foil pouches & 25kg PP woven export bags.
* **Regulation:** Export permitted under seasonal quota allocations issued by the Ministry of Commerce.

### 3. Pesticide-Free Traditional Dried Fish (Shukti)
* **Key Species:** Silver Pomfret (Rupchanda), Ribbonfish (Chhuri), Dried Shrimp (Chingri), Bombay Duck (Loitta).
* **Processing SOP:** Solar dome dehydrator cured; zero synthetic chemical insecticides (DDT/Malathion free).
* **Export Standards:** Moisture <= 15%, Histamine < 200 ppm, Total Volatile Base Nitrogen (TVBN) within Codex Stan 167-1989.
* **Packaging:** Food-grade nitrogen-flushed vacuum pouches.

### 4. Raw & Roasted Peanuts / Value-Added Nut Products
* **Origin:** Char regions of Northern Bangladesh & Padma basin.
* **Export Standards:** Total Aflatoxin < 4 ppb (B1+B2+G1+G2) matching strict EU Regulation 1881/2006; Moisture 7-8%.
* **Value-Add Lines:** Crunchy & Smooth 100% Pure Peanut Butter (zero palm oil, zero added sugar).

### 5. Fresh Fruits & Horticultural Specialties
* **Core Commodities:** Himsagar / Khirsapat Mango (Rajshahi - GI), Green Jackfruit (Kathal).
* **Mandatory Post-Harvest Protocol:** Hot Water Treatment (HWT: 47°C for 75 min) to eliminate Oriental Fruit Fly eggs/larvae; rapid precooling to 13°C; active cold-chain transit to airport.

## ⚖️ PART 3: BANGLADESH EXPORT LEGAL & BANKING WORKFLOW

1. **Trade License** -> Local City Corporation/Municipality.
2. **e-TIN** -> National Board of Revenue (NBR) Portal.
3. **BIN (VAT Registration)** -> 13-digit code on vat.gov.bd.
4. **AD Bank Current Account** -> Authorized Dealer in Foreign Exchange (BRAC Bank, EBL, Bank Asia, Dutch-Bangla).
5. **Chamber Membership** -> DCCI / CCCI / relevant regional trade chamber.
6. **Export Registration Certificate (ERC)** -> Export Promotion Bureau (EPB).
7. **EXP Form & Clearance** -> Bangladesh Bank FX declaration filed prior to customs port gate-in.
8. **Proceeds Realization Certificate (PRC)** -> Issued by AD bank upon foreign currency realization within 120 days.
9. **Government Cash Incentives (নগদ প্রণোদনা):** 5% to 20% FOB reimbursement claimed through AD bank via PRC and EPB documentation.
