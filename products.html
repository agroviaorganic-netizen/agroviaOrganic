Chemical-Free Solar Dome Processing

Traditional open-air drying often involves unauthorized organochlorine insecticides (DDT/Malathion) to prevent fly infestation. Agrovia Organic utilizes modern enclosed solar hybrid poly-dome dehydrators with filtered air circulation—guaranteeing 100% pesticide-free, hygienic curing.

Key Species & Laboratory Benchmarks

Available Species: Rupchanda, Chhuri, Loitta, Chingri
Chemical Pesticides: Zero Detected (GC-MS Verified)
Histamine Concentration: < 200 ppm (Codex Spec)
Moisture Range: ≤ 15.0%

Packaging & Quality Controls

  • Barrier Pouches: 100g, 250g, 500g Nitrogen-flushed heavy multi-layer pouches.
  • Master Carton: 10kg corrugated export cartons with desiccants.
  • Odor & Freshness: Sealed vacuum prevents aroma leakage during air/sea transit.
  • Health Certificate: Dept. of Fisheries (DoF) export health certificate.

Himsagar Mango (GI), Khirsapat & Green Jackfruit

Origin: Rajshahi, Chapainawabganj & Meherpur Certified Orchards

Book Air Cargo Allocation →

Mandatory Hot Water Quarantine & Fruit Bagging

To fulfill strict European Union (EU) and UK phytosanitary import regulations concerning *Bactrocera* fruit flies, our mangoes are tree-bagged 40 days prior to harvest and undergo automated **Hot Water Treatment (HWT: 47°C for 75 min)** at government-approved facilities in Dhaka.

Post-Harvest & Quality Specifications

Varietal Availability: Himsagar (May-Jun), Amrapali (Jun-Jul)
Sugar / Brix Level: 19° - 22° Brix (Peak Sweetness)
Pre-Cooling Target: Rapid drop to 13°C post-HWT
Export Fruit Weight: 220g - 350g per individual fruit

Cold Chain & Air Cargo Logistics

  • Departure Gateway: Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC).
  • Packaging: 2kg, 4kg, 5kg ventilated corrugated export cartons with foam netting.
  • Active Cold Transit: Reefer truck from orchard to airport cargo village.
  • Inspection: Phytosanitary Inspection Certificate issued at airport.
