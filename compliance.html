Lab Standards & Regulatory Compliance

Comprehensive testing protocols ensuring zero pesticide residues, zero C4 sugar adulteration, mycotoxin control, and flawless pre-shipment inspection.

The 4-Stage Agrovia Quality Gate

Every kilogram of commodity passes through four strict verification barriers prior to port release.

1

Farm-Gate Intake Audit

Direct harvest verification, raw moisture measurement, and preliminary organoleptic batch screening.

2

Lab Chemical Analysis

IRMS isotope analysis for C4 sugars, HPLC for aflatoxins, and GC-MS for pesticide residues.

3

Controlled Packaging & Fumigation

Sortex optical sorting, vacuum nitrogen-flush packing, and Phostoxin gas quarantine treatment.

4

PSI & Authority Sign-Off

Pre-shipment inspection (SGS/BV) alongside Department of Agricultural Extension Phytosanitary clearance.
