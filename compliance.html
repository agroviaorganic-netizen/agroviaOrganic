Lab Standards & QA Framework

Our 4-stage quality gate ensures every shipment meets ISO 22000, HACCP, SGS, Halal, and EU food safety standards — with full documentation available to qualified buyers.

4-Stage Quality Gate

Every batch passes four consecutive quality checkpoints before export clearance is granted.

🌱
Stage 1

Farm-Level Audit

GPS-tagged field inspection, pesticide usage review, soil testing for heavy metals. Farmer compliance agreement signed.

🔬
Stage 2

Accredited Lab Testing

Representative samples tested at BSTI / SGS accredited labs — EA-IRMS (honey), HPLC (aflatoxin), GC-MS (pesticides), histamine ELISA.

📦
Stage 3

Pre-Shipment Inspection

SGS or Bureau Veritas inspection of packed consignment. Quantity verification, packing check, sampling, document review.

✈️
Stage 4

Export Documentation

Full documentary pack: COO, Phytosanitary, Halal Cert, SGS Report, CoA, BL/AWB, Packing List, Commercial Invoice.
