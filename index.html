Scientific Quality Assurance

Zero Adulteration, Zero Chemical Residues, Guaranteed.

We eliminate border quarantine risk and customs rejections. Every batch is certified with complete laboratory testing covering pesticide residues, adulteration, mycotoxins, and quarantine requirements.

Detects C4 sugar syrups (cane and corn) at microscopic isotopic fractions, guaranteeing 0.000% raw honey adulteration.
