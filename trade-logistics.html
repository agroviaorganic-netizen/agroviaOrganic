FOB (Free on Board)

Agrovia delivers goods loaded on board the vessel at Chattogram Sea Port (BDCGP) or loaded into aircraft at DAC. Exporter covers all inland transport, export customs, EXP filing, and terminal handling charges (THC). Buyer manages ocean/air freight and marine insurance.

CFR (Cost and Freight)

Agrovia arranges and pre-pays ocean freight up to your specified port of discharge (e.g., CFR London Gateway, CFR Jebel Ali). Risk transfers once goods pass the ship’s rail at origin port. Buyer arranges marine insurance.

CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)

Comprehensive turnkey solution where Agrovia covers cost, international freight, and Institute Cargo Clauses (A) All-Risk marine insurance up to the buyer’s destination port. Recommended for hassle-free procurement.

Trade Finance & Banking Security (UCP 600)

Structured foreign currency payments through Authorized Dealer (AD) banks in Bangladesh.

Irrevocable Confirmed Sight LC

Ideal for large commercial orders (+). Issued by tier-1 buyer bank and advised/confirmed through top foreign exchange banks in Bangladesh (BRAC Bank, Eastern Bank Ltd, Bank Asia). Strictly audited under ICC UCP 600 guidelines to eliminate non-compliance discrepancies.

50% Advance T/T + 50% CAD (against B/L)

Standard payment structure for initial or boutique trial shipments ( – ). 50% advance bank wire transfer for inventory aggregation and 50% balance against clean scanned Bill of Lading (B/L) and original lab COA package.

Bangladesh Regulatory Export Workflow

1. Regulatory Registrations

ERC (Export Registration Certificate from EPB), e-TIN, 13-digit BIN, and DCCI Chamber Membership.

2. FX Declaration (EXP Form)

Electronic EXP Form issued by Authorized Dealer Bank prior to customs gate-in clearance.

3. Proceeds Realization (PRC)

PRC issued within 120 days of export value repatriation, qualifying for 5%-20% national agro-incentive.
