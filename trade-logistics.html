Incoterms 2020 Options

FOBFree on Board — Chittagong

Seller responsible until goods are on board the vessel at Chittagong. Buyer arranges freight and insurance. Most common for experienced importers.

CFRCost & Freight

Agrovia covers cost and freight to destination port. Buyer arranges marine insurance. Risk transfers at origin port.

CIFCost, Insurance & Freight

Full coverage arrangement: Agrovia covers freight + marine insurance to buyer's named port. Recommended for first-time buyers.
